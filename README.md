## ✨ Img2Img Features

- **Reference-guided editing**: Begin with an existing subject, composition, pose, shape, or layout instead of relying on text alone.

- **Controlled visual changes**: Restyle an image, replace a background, relight a portrait, explore another material palette, or create a fresh variation.

- **Multiple-reference workflows**: Add ordered references when the selected model supports them and assign each image a role such as subject, scene, lighting, material, or style.

- **Flexible output controls**: Choose an available model, optional Style, aspect ratio, supported quality or resolution, and the plan-supported number of outputs.

- **Upfront credit information**: Review the displayed credit reservation before generation. Credits are kept only for successfully delivered outputs, while the unused portion is returned when an output is unavailable.

- **Continue from any result**: Review delivered images in the workspace or Creations, download a result, or reuse it as a reference for the next edit.

## 🛠️ How to Use Image-to-Image AI

Follow this four-step workflow, or read the live guide to [how image-to-image AI works](https://img2img.net/#how-it-works):

1. **Upload your references**: Add one or more images you have the right to use and decide what each image contributes.
2. **Describe the change**: State what must remain recognizable first, then describe the background, lighting, color, material, mood, or style you want.
3. **Choose the model and settings**: Select the available model, optional Style, ratio, quality, and output count, then review the displayed credit cost.
4. **Generate, compare, and continue**: Let the task run in the background, inspect the delivered results, download an image, or use it as the next reference.

## 🎨 Popular Img2Img Workflows

- **Product backgrounds**: Keep a product recognizable while placing it in a cleaner ecommerce, lifestyle, or campaign scene.
- **Portrait relighting**: Change the mood and lighting while preserving important identity, expression, hair, clothing, and framing details.
- **Interior redesign**: Explore another time of day, material palette, or decor direction without discarding the room structure.
- **Illustration and anime styles**: Transform a portrait or scene into a more graphic visual treatment while retaining the intended composition.
- **Sketch to concept**: Develop a rough idea with more believable materials, geometry, depth, and light.
- **Seasonal scenes**: Change weather and atmosphere while keeping the place and camera perspective recognizable.

## 💡 Tips for Better Img2Img Results

- Give every reference image one clear purpose.
- Say what must be preserved before describing what should change.
- Use concrete details for lighting, materials, background, framing, and mood.
- Keep the first attempt focused on one visual direction.
- Iterate from the strongest delivered result instead of rewriting everything at once.
- Review the selected model, settings, and credit quote before starting.
- Expect results to vary with the source images, prompt, reference order, model, and settings.
